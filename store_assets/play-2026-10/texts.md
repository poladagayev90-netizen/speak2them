## 🇬🇧 English (en-US)

**Title** (max 30)
```
SpeakLab.az
```

**Short description** (max 80)
```
Planned English speaking practice with real partners, plus AI feedback.
```

**Full description** (max 4000)
```
SpeakLab is where you learn to speak English by speaking it. Planned voice calls with real people, personal AI feedback after every call, and a new topic every day.

📅 PLANNED PRACTICE
Tell us when you are free and how many times a week you want to speak. You are offered a set time with a partner at your level; you confirm, and the practice goes into your week. Today shows your next session, the Plan tab shows your whole week, and changing the time or messaging your partner is one tap.

🗣️ A TOPIC READY FOR EVERY CALL
A new topic every day: travel, work, technology, family, city life and more. Words and idioms with their meaning in Azerbaijani and Turkish, easy and hard questions, a quiz, pictures and short videos to describe. Calls have activities too: describe a picture, Taboo, debate.

🧪 AI FEEDBACK AFTER EVERY CALL
After a call your speaking is analysed and the report arrives in your own language: your mistakes with corrections, new words, speaking pace, and scores for fluency, grammar and vocabulary. In Lab you see each session on its own and your progress week by week in charts.

🤖 TALK TO AInur ANY TIME
When you want to speak without waiting for a partner, the AI teacher AInur is always ready: describe pictures and videos, free talk, debate. The same detailed report after every session.

👩‍🏫 ONE-TO-ONE LESSONS WITH A TEACHER
If you take lessons with a teacher, each lesson's time and topic are in the app in advance. Before the lesson you prepare the topic, the words and the story, and you get a reminder; after it, homework built from your own mistakes.

📚 HOW TO PRACTISE
17 short lessons: how to describe a picture, how to keep a conversation going, ready phrases for Taboo and debate.

The app's interface is in English, so every screen is practice too. AI reports and word meanings are in Azerbaijani or Turkish.

speaklab.az
```
