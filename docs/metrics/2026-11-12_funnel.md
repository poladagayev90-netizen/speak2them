## The 3 biggest drop-offs

1. **Booked → attended: 8 → 0.** Every new learner who got a booking missed it. Seven of the nine attendance rows are their own `no_show`. This is the whole North-star problem in one step: the plan produces bookings, but newcomers don't turn up.
2. **Signup → onboarding: 20 → 11 (45 % lost).** Nine people made an account and never finished the wizard (11 steps, including the week grid).
3. **Platform cannot pair: 22 `unmatched` slots in 30 days**, almost twice the 12 attended. In weeks with no match the North-star metric drops to 0 (Oct 12 and Oct 26).

The warm-up (1 of 3 eligible) is too new to judge: it shipped on Nov 7, and nobody has signed up since Nov 9.

## Three fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Make the newcomer's first call a host call within 48 h, booked inside onboarding.**
   - *Why first:* this targets drop-off 1 (0 of 8 attended) and drop-off 3 at the same time.
   - *What:* the host is Polad (memory `newcomer-host-decisions`: his hours only, not his own students). The last onboarding step offers 2–3 of the host's slots; the learner picks one.
   - *Why it should work:* a newcomer paired with another newcomer needs two people to show up. Both no-shows and unmatched slots come from that.
   - *Measure:* booked → attended for the next 10 signups. Target: ≥ 50 % (today 0 %).
2. **"Still on?" check-in 3 h before every newcomer's booking, with release and re-offer.**
   - *What:* one push asks the learner to tap «I'll be there». No tap 60 min before means the slot is released: the partner gets "your partner can't make it" and is offered Online now or the next slot. Today the partner waits and both get an attendance row.
   - *Builds on:* the 1 h / 10 min reminders that went live on Nov 10–11. They tell people about the call but don't ask them to confirm it.
   - *Measure:* the newcomer `no_show` share (today 7 of 9 rows).
3. **Shorten onboarding to what pairing needs, and count each step.**
   - *What:* level, 2–3 free time blocks and the charter come first. Goal, topics, "maybe" times and country move to an optional "tell us more" card on Today.
   - *Also:* write the furthest step reached to `onboarding/{uid}.stepReached`. Right now we can't tell which of the 11 steps loses the 9 people.
   - *Measure:* signup → onboarded. Target: ≥ 75 % (today 55 %).

Not ranked, but worth noting: the teacher channel brought **0 of 20** new learners in this window. The 16 linked learners all joined earlier. If the teacher pilot is the growth plan, the invite link has not produced a learner in 30 days.
